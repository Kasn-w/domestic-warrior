# Domestic Warrior
*Built October 2026*

There is rat infestation in the area, and as the Domestic Warrior, you can't allow it. You're going to kill every single one of them and then take down their KING!

![gameplay screenshot](_forREADME/preview.gif)

## About
Built solo with [Godot 4.6.2](https://godotengine.org/) in 72 hours for [Mini Jam 222: Enchanted](https://itch.io/jam/mini-jam-222-enchanted), under the theme "Enchanted" and Limitation "Cat".



## How to Play
- Control
    - `WASD` to Move
    - `Left Mouse` to Claw swipe
    - `Right Mouse` to Dash
    - `Hold Left Mouse` to Bite
    - `E` to Sleep

- Goal
    - Defeat the Rat KING!
